# Cover Emails to School Placement Offices
### *The Wolf of McKenzie Towne · Student placement outreach*

**Before sending:**
- Attach **Student-Placement-Postings.pdf** to every email. The **Wolf-of-McKenzie-Towne-Invitation.pdf** is an optional second attachment.
- Replace every **[bracketed]** field.
- Send by **early November** so students can be matched before the January term.
- One email per school. Personalize the first line if you know the coordinator's name.

## 1 · Mount Royal University: Community Service Learning

**To:** adc@mtroyal.ca (Academic Development Centre, Community Service Learning)
**Subject:** Community Service Learning partner request: youth-led community theatre production (Winter 2027)

> Dear Community Service Learning team,
>
> I'm writing on behalf of the Youth Race Council in McKenzie Towne, Calgary SE, to ask about becoming a Community Service Learning partner for the Winter 2027 term.
>
> We're producing *The Wolf of McKenzie Towne*, a community-wide theatre production staged as an Amazing Race. Next August, teens will race through eight themed neighbourhood parks to recover a stolen lantern, ending in a dusk finale where the whole community turns on its porch lights. The event is planned and run by a council of 10–12 teens, supported by screened adult volunteers.
>
> We're looking for students who can **mentor those young leaders** in two areas:
> - **Theatre:** direction and character coaching, or design and stage management
> - **Marketing and communications:** campaign and publicity, or partnerships and sponsorship
>
> The commitment fits the CSL format: about **3–5 hours a week**, centred on one weekly evening planning session, for the Winter term, with an option to continue through race day in late August. Each mentor has a named on-site supervisor, and we'll complete whatever learning plans and evaluations your program requires. A season honorarium is planned, subject to funding.
>
> Students will come away with real portfolio work: a live campaign, a staged production, design and build credits, and a reference letter. They'll also have made a visible difference for youth in their city.
>
> The attached postings describe each role. Could you tell me how to register as a community partner, and whether these roles could be matched with students in communications, marketing, business or the performing arts?
>
> Thank you for considering it. I'd welcome a short call at your convenience.
>
> Warm regards,
> [Your name]
> [Role], on behalf of the Youth Race Council, McKenzie Towne
> [Phone] · [Email]

### Tips
- **Find a named person.** A short call to each school's main line ("Who handles community partners for placements?") greatly improves response rates.
- **Mention the host organization** once MTCA or MTC formally agrees to host. Schools often need a registered organization on the placement agreement.
- **Keep a simple tracker** with the school, contact, date sent, follow-up date and status.
